Why this exists

A personal newspaper for a fuller London life.

Brian's London Daily News was created by Brian Groth to make the most of living near Hampstead Heath and working in the City—without beginning every morning with a maze of tabs, feeds and generic headlines.

01

London, edited to be useful

Each edition turns live reporting, official sources and an automated RSS candidate list into ten concise choices, two apiece across five themes: something near home, something near work, a London AI development, a London technology advancement and one item worth planning ahead for.

The goal is not to reproduce the news. It is to answer a more personal question: what is worth knowing or doing today?

02

Built as a living collaboration

Brian shaped the purpose, priorities and reading experience. OpenAI Codex researches, cross-checks, writes, categorises, builds and tests each daily edition under Brian’s direction.

GitHub is the repository and publishing backbone: it keeps the source, preserves the history of each improvement and serves the finished static newspaper to the web.

The editorial promise

Local first

Hampstead Heath and the EC2N working day receive genuine priority.

Decision-ready

Built to evolve

The format improves as Brian learns what makes London more rewarding.
